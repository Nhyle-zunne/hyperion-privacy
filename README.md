# Privacy Policy for Hyperion Maritime CRP

**Effective Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Application Identifier:** com.nhyle.hyperionmaritime  
**Developer Contact:** zagfunne@gmail.com  

Hyperion Maritime ("we", "our", or "us") provides Hyperion Maritime CRP as an offline-first reference and exam review terminal for maritime deck officers and radio operators preparing for OIC-NW and GMDSS licensure examinations.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Storage
* **Local Storage Only:** All exam practice sessions, quiz scores, question bookmarks, and drill configurations are stored locally on your device via internal Room/SQLite database storage.
* **No Account Creation:** You are not required to create an account, register an email, or submit personal identifiers to access reference materials.
* **Clearance Tokens:** Verification tokens used for institutional seat activation are validated locally or via direct encrypted connection solely to confirm feature entitlement.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Network Access
* **Zero Tracking SDKs:** The application does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or telemetry analytics tools.
* **External Support:** The application includes an optional external link to an institutional candidate support desk via Messenger. Any interaction through that service is subject to Meta's privacy terms.

---

### 3. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all candidate progress is saved solely on your local device storage:
* You maintain complete control over your records.
* Clearing application data in your Android system settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored test results and history.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Hyperion Maritime CRP is strictly designed for maritime deck professionals and licensure candidates aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

### 5. Policy Updates
We may update this policy periodically to align with application features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be reflected directly on this hosted page.
