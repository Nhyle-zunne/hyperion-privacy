**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026 | **Last Updated:** October 26, 2026

Hyperion Maritime ("Hyperion," "we," "our," or "us") provides Hyperion Maritime Reviewer, an examination-preparation service for maritime deck officers, marine engineers, radio operators, and other eligible candidates. It is available as an Android application (the "Android App") and as a website and Progressive Web Application (the "Website"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services.

## 1. Summary

- The **Android App has no sign-in and no user accounts.** Your study progress is stored only on your device.
- The **Website/PWA lets you sign in with Google or Facebook** through Supabase Auth.
- Android purchases are handled by **Google Play Billing**. We never see your payment card details.
- Licensing and promotional codes are checked online through Google Firebase services.
- We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.
- You can delete your Website account at any time (see Section 8).

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Android App

**Study data stored on your device only.** Practice and mock exam progress, competency drill answers, bookmarks, mistakes notebook, study time, and app preferences stay in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data and cannot access, recover, or delete it for you. You can remove it at any time through Android Settings → Apps → Hyperion Maritime → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

**Google Play purchases.** When you buy or restore a subscription or access pass, Google Play Billing processes the transaction. Our app and licensing service may receive or process your Google Play order ID, product and subscription identifiers, purchase token, purchase time, and subscription status, so that we can verify the purchase, grant the correct access, and prevent fraud. Google handles all payment details under its own terms and privacy policy.

**Promotional codes and license checks.** When you enter a promotional code or the app verifies a license online, we may process: the code or license reference you submit, a device or terminal identifier used for license verification, the app package name and version, device manufacturer/model and Android version, and the validation result and timestamps. This identifier is pseudonymous, but we treat it as personal information because it may be linked to a device over time.

**What the Android App does not do.** The Android App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location. It does not contain third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

### 2.2 Website and PWA

When you use the Website or PWA, we may process:

- **Sign-in information** if you choose Google or Facebook sign-in: email address, display name, profile picture (if provided by the provider), a unique provider account identifier, and authentication session data. We never receive your Google or Facebook password.
- **Account and license data:** your Hyperion account identifier, license status, and access entitlements.
- **Study data** that a Website feature saves or synchronizes to your account, if any.
- **Browser storage** (localStorage, cookies, or similar) needed to keep you signed in and remember settings. Clearing it may sign you out or reset local preferences.
- **Technical data** (such as IP address and browser type) processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver and secure the Website.

### 2.3 Support Communications

If you contact us by email, Meta Messenger, or another channel, we process the information you send (contact details, account reference, message content, troubleshooting details) to respond to you and keep reasonable support records.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

1. Create and manage Website accounts, authenticate you, and maintain sessions.
2. Deliver the examination content and study features you request.
3. Verify purchases, restore entitlements, validate promotional codes, and manage access to examination tracks.
4. Prevent fraud, misuse of promotional codes, and attempts to bypass license restrictions.
5. Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot our services.
6. Respond to support requests.
7. Comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

We do not use Google or Facebook sign-in to access any information in your social accounts beyond what is listed above. We do not use your information for profiling, behavioral advertising, or automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 4. Legal Bases (for users in the EEA, UK, and similar regions)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on: **performance of a contract** (providing accounts, licenses, and purchased access), **legitimate interests** (security, fraud prevention, service reliability), **legal obligation** (tax, accounting, and legal requests), and **consent** where we ask for it separately, which you may withdraw at any time.

## 5. Service Providers and Data Sharing

We share information only with providers that help us run the service, and only as needed:

- **Supabase** (Website authentication and backend): https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Google** (Google sign-in on the Website; Google Play Billing; Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Firestore for configuration, promotional validation, and license verification): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Meta** (Facebook Login on the Website): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
- **Hosting, content delivery, and security providers** for the Website.

We may also disclose information (a) where required by law or valid legal process, (b) to investigate fraud or protect users and our systems, and (c) to a successor if our business or assets are transferred, subject to applicable law.

**We do not sell personal information** and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes. Each provider above also processes data under its own privacy policy.

## 6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Hyperion does not display third-party ads and does not use advertising identifiers for tracking. The Android App does not include third-party analytics SDKs. The Website uses only storage and cookies that are essential for sign-in, settings, and security. If we ever add non-essential analytics or tracking, we will update this policy first and obtain consent where the law requires it.

## 8. Your Choices, Deletion, and Rights

### 8.1 Deleting your Website account and data

You can delete your Hyperion Website account in two ways:

- **From the Website:** Settings → Account → Delete Account.
- **Without signing in or installing anything:** https://hyperionmaritime.org/delete-account, or email hyperionmaritimex@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account."

Deletion is permanent. It is not a temporary deactivation. We delete or de-identify your profile, sign-in identifiers, license links, and any synced study data within [30] days of a verified request, except limited records we must keep as described in Section 7. Deleting your account may remove your ability to sign in or restore account-linked access. If you used Facebook Login, you can also remove Hyperion under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

### 8.2 Android App data

Because the Android App has no accounts and keeps study data only on your device, you control that data directly: Android Settings → Apps → Hyperion Maritime → Storage → Clear data. If you want license-verification records associated with your device identifier or promotional code removed, email us and include the code or the information needed to locate it. Google Play purchase records are held by Google and are managed through your Google account.

### 8.3 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to erase or block, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to claim compensation for damages from unlawful processing. This includes your rights under the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, the **GDPR/UK GDPR**, and, for California residents, the **CCPA/CPRA** (we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define the terms, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights).

To exercise a right, email hyperionmaritimex@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting, and we respond within [30] days. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or payment card details.

### 8.4 Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the **National Privacy Commission of the Philippines** (https://privacy.gov.ph) or to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including HTTPS/TLS for network communications, authentication and session controls, access restrictions on backend data, Android's application sandbox for local data, and license-verification controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify affected users and the appropriate authorities as required by law.

## 10. International Processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States and other countries where they operate. Where required, such transfers rely on the providers' safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Hyperion Maritime Reviewer is intended for adults and candidates preparing for maritime licensing. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email hyperionmaritimex@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 12. Subscriptions and Cancellation

Android subscriptions are managed by Google Play. You can manage or cancel them at: **Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Cancelling a subscription does not delete locally stored study data or any Website account. If a license is revoked, access to the affected features changes according to the applicable license terms.
